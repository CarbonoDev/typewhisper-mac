How it works

Before the call, during it, and long after.

Meeting Whisper reads your calendar, so it knows a meeting is coming and what happened last time you met these people.

01Before

Walk in already briefed.

  • Pre-meeting briefSummaries of prior meetings with the same people or series, plus relevant passages from your vault, drafted into one page.
  • On a schedule or on demandBriefs can be generated automatically ahead of time, or the moment you ask for one.
  • Degrades gracefullyNo vault? It uses prior meetings. No prior meetings? It uses your notes.
02During

Record from the menu bar, keep working.

  • Nothing gets lostStabilized transcript text is written to disk as the meeting runs, so an interruption costs you nothing.
  • Ask mid-callQuestion the transcript so far. If your own material doesn't answer it, the model may consult your vault once — and says so.
  • Scheduled or ad-hocLet an upcoming calendar event prompt you, or hit record for a call nobody booked.
03After

The recording becomes a document you keep.

  • Clean final transcriptOn stop, the full audio is re-transcribed into timestamped segments — better than the live pass.
  • Summary and analysisStructured outputs from editable templates. Summary and Extended Analysis ship ready to use.
  • Into your vaultExport as Markdown with frontmatter and a backlink, in a layout that never overwrites an existing note.

Speaker labeling

Guessing is the last resort, not the first.

Who said what is decided after the call, by whichever source is most trustworthy. The first rung that applies wins — a detection model only runs when nothing more certain is available.

Where your meetings live

On your Mac, in your files, under your license.

Every part of the pipeline has an on-device path. Use a cloud API when you want the speed — that is a setting, not an architecture.

Transcription

Local engines, no account

WhisperKit, Parakeet TDT v3, Apple SpeechAnalyzer, and MLX-based Voxtral, Qwen3 and Granite all run on your machine. Groq, OpenAI, xAI and others are there if you prefer them.

Language models

Per-purpose routing

Briefs, summaries, Q&A and language detection each pick their own provider and model. Point the sensitive ones at local Gemma 4 and the rest at whatever is fastest.

Storage

Plain files you own

Audio and meeting data sit in Application Support on your disk. Exports are ordinary Markdown in the Obsidian vault you already keep.

Automation

Localhost only, off by default

The REST API binds to 127.0.0.1 and stays disabled until you turn it on. The CLI is a thin client of it — good for bulk-importing years of old transcripts.

Get it running

Clone it, open it, hit build.

There is no signed release yet — you build it yourself. Swift package dependencies resolve on their own and signing is ad-hoc, so nothing needs configuring first.

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Xcode 16+
  • Apple Silicon recommended; 16 GB RAM for the larger local models
  • Grant microphone, calendar and accessibility access on first launch
  • Local pyannote diarization additionally wants Python 3 with pyannote.audio
